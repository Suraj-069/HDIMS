How HDIMS Keeps You in Control

Healthcare data shouldn't be scattered across WhatsApp, paper files, and hospital portals. Here's how HDIMS organizes your journey.

01

Unified Digital Vault

Upload past reports, prescriptions, scans, and discharge summaries for yourself and your entire family. Easily search by condition, year, or family member.

02

Time-Boxed Consent

When consulting a doctor, choose exactly which records to share and for how long (e.g. 1 hour). Authorize via a secure 6-digit OTP that auto-expires.

03

AI Health Summaries

HDIMS synthesizes multi-year lab reports into a concise, plain-language health timeline — helping you and your physician review history in 60 seconds.

Enterprise Capability

Designed for Indian Healthcare Realities

Built for how families manage care across hospitals, multiple generations, specialist referrals, and emergency walk-ins.

Family Health Vault

Organize records for children, spouses, parents, and elderly grandparents under a single login. Switch profiles in one click.

Granular Shared Access

Never give open-ended record access. Grant specific doctors access to selected records with live countdown timers and instant revocation.

AI Clinical Insights

Complex medical jargon turned into chronological clinical timelines, highlighting allergies, past surgeries, and recurring diagnostics.

Walk-In Emergency OTP

Visiting a clinic without prior appointment? Generate an instant 6-digit access code from your phone so the clinician can unlock records immediately.

Seamless Appointments

Browse verified specialist directories, select available morning or afternoon slots, and get real-time status updates.

Digital Prescriptions

Access every issued prescription with exact dosage schedules, refill alerts, and doctor remarks in high-resolution digital format.

Bank-Grade Compliance

Your data is encrypted. Your permission is final.

HDIMS operates on zero-knowledge patient consent. No hospital, doctor, or third party can view medical data without explicit, time-boxed authorization verified through your secure phone OTP.
